Book One

The Fall Before Flight

A new home, an offer of power, and a grave mistake.

Aria and Nala live unhappily with their adoptive parents, who only want coin out of Aria and prestige out of Nala. As Aria navigates forced labor she meets a little boy thief, who becomes her door to the Unwanteds: a group of youth who live, train, and rebel together from an underground cave connected to a green river. Upon discovering her parent’s plans to marry off Nala for status, Aria convinces her sister to join her in running away to live with the Unwanteds, but finds her new home not as welcoming as she expected.
