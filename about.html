About

Liza Beth

An avid reader from childhood, Liza spent much of her time with her head in the clouds as she rode dragons or her feet underground as she riddled in the dark with unhappy creatures who had lost their “precious.” As an adult she considers this time well spent, as the values of courage, integrity, and standing up for those who are weak have followed her into the real world, where she mentors and teaches young people not unlike herself.

Now with stories of her own to share, lots to say about the ones she’s read, and many people in front of her that she wants to share them with, Liza Beth hopes that you find the worlds and words from her head inspiring to young and old folk alike. For stories are how we learn, about the world and about ourselves, and so their existence, as well as their caliber, matter.
